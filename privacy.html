Privacy Policy

Applies to lifeprepacademyfoundation.com. Last updated: February 2, 2026 .

Overview

LifePrep Academy Foundation supports community programs serving Baltimore, Maryland and Paducah, Kentucky . This page explains how we handle information you share with us.

Information We Collect

  • Contact form submissions : name, email, subject, and message.
  • Anti-spam/verification : we use Cloudflare Turnstile on the contact form to reduce automated abuse.
  • Basic technical data : your browser may send standard information such as IP address and user agent to our form processor.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to inquiries about programs, partnerships, sponsorships, and volunteering.
  • Protect the site from spam and abuse.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers strictly to operate this website (for example, form processing and anti-spam verification).

Retention

We keep contact requests as long as needed to respond and for organizational recordkeeping.

Your Choices

You can use the form on our Contact page to request updates or deletion of your message where feasible.

Contact

Use the form on our Contact page to reach us.
