NFL FLAG Program

NFL FLAG gives youth athletes a fast-paced, non-contact football experience focused on fundamentals, confidence, and teamwork. Explore the program highlights below and register when your family is ready.

LifePrep Academy Foundation NFL FLAG programming creates a positive environment where athletes learn football skills, game awareness, leadership, and sportsmanship through age-appropriate instruction and structured play.

  • Ages 4-14
  • Non-contact format
  • All skill levels welcome
  • Structured instruction
  • Team-first culture
  • Athlete-safety focus

What Families Can Expect

Skill-building first

Players develop core skills including route running, catching, defensive positioning, and football IQ.

Game-ready confidence

Drills and scrimmage situations help athletes grow decision-making and confidence under pressure.

Positive team culture

Coaches reinforce communication, effort, and respect so athletes can compete and support each other.

Inclusive participation

Program sessions are designed to keep athletes engaged, active, and developing each week.

Built for Emerging Athletes

NFL FLAG creates a fun on-ramp for players new to football while still challenging returning athletes to grow.

Focus: movement, fundamentals, and confidence from day one.

Structured Training, Real Progress

Sessions are organized around repeatable skill work, football concepts, and controlled game play.

Every session builds discipline, teamwork, and game sense.

Community-Centered Experience

Families, coaches, and supporters help shape a program where athletes feel encouraged and accountable.

Character development matters as much as athletic growth.

Our Commitment to Player Safety

LifePrep Academy Foundation NFL FLAG programming is built on a positive, inclusive, and safety-first environment. Coaches, volunteers, and supporting adults are expected to follow athlete-protection procedures and event-day safety standards.

  • Non-contact rules and age-appropriate structure
  • Weather and heat-safety protocols for training and games
  • Emergency action planning and response readiness
  • Clear sideline behavior expectations for athletes and families

Additional safety details are published through registration and season communications.

Program announcements, scheduling updates, and event details are sent through the registration pathway.
