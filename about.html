About LifePrep Academy Foundation

At LifePrep Academy Foundation, we are dedicated to providing mental health resources, athletic training, financial literacy workshops, and youth advancement opportunities. Our website serves as a platform to raise awareness and facilitate donations, supporting our mission to empower individuals in Baltimore, Maryland and Paducah, Kentucky.

We focus on practical, hands‑on learning, trusted mentorship, and accessible resources so every participant can take the next step with confidence.

Our Mission

To provide opportunities for underserved communities by promoting both mental and physical health awareness, mentorship and financial literacy.

We pursue this mission by meeting learners where they are, building supportive relationships, and connecting them to clear pathways in school, work, and life.

Our Offerings

Life Prep Academy offers various services that help to drive our mission. If you are interested in any of the services listed below, feel free to reach out to us by using the form included on the Contact Us page. We look forward to working with you!

  • Interactive workshops and school assemblies
  • Small‑group coaching and mentorship circles
  • Team clinics and athletic development sessions
  • Family nights and community engagement events
