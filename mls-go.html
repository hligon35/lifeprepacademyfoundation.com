MLS GO Program

MLS GO is a fun, community-centered soccer experience designed to help young athletes build confidence, learn the game, and enjoy a season of structured team play. Families can register now for our fall experience with more program details coming soon.

Register for MLS GO Volunteer Form Coaching Application

Paducah GO Soccer is an official MLS GO youth program, locally operated by LifePrep Academy Foundation. This season is built to give families a clear, welcoming path into youth soccer with organized divisions, dedicated coaches, weekly practices, and Saturday game-day energy for every player.

  • Draft Party: September 16 at Jetton
  • First Practice: September 23
  • Season Opener: September 27
  • $75 per player
  • All skill levels welcome
  • More details coming soon
